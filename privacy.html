Privacy Policy

Jause Plano · Effective September 8, 2026 · Versión en español

Who is responsible

Adrián Sosa, individual developer of the Jause Plano app. Contact: adriansosa267@gmail.com.

Summary

Jause Plano does not collect, transmit or store personal data on servers. Everything you scan stays on your iPhone only.

What data the app handles

  • Scans and floor plans. When you scan a room, the app creates a floor plan, a 3D model and the related measurements. These files are saved in the app’s private storage on your device.
  • Preferences. The app remembers whether you have already seen the introduction. That is the only setting it stores.

The app does not ask for your name, email address, phone number or any other identifying information. There is no sign-up and no account.

Use of the camera and LiDAR sensor

The camera and LiDAR sensor are used only to recognize walls, doors and windows while you scan, in order to create the floor plan. All processing happens on the device using operating system technology. No photos or video are recorded or sent. You can revoke camera access at any time in iOS Settings; the app will no longer be able to scan, but you keep the floor plans you already have.

Who the data is shared with

No one. The app includes no analytics, advertising or tracking tools, and it does not communicate with its own servers or third-party servers. No data is sold or transferred.

If you choose to share a floor plan or 3D model using the iOS share feature, that file travels through the channel you pick, such as email or messaging. That transfer is governed by the privacy policy of the service you use.

How long data is kept

Floor plans remain on your device until you delete them. You can delete a single floor plan from its detail screen, or all of them at once from Settings inside the app. If you uninstall the app, iOS deletes all of its data.

If iCloud Backup is turned on, your floor plans may be included in your device’s encrypted backup, which is managed by Apple and which the developer cannot access.

Children

The app is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect data from anyone, regardless of age.

Your rights

Because all data is on your device and under your control, you can access, export and delete it at any time from within the app, without having to ask. If you have questions about this policy, write to adriansosa267@gmail.com.

Changes to this policy

If the app ever adds features that handle data differently, this page will be updated and the effective date above will change before those features become available.
