What the app does

Jause Plano uses the iPhone’s LiDAR sensor to create an approximate floor plan of a room, with its area, perimeter, height and the position of doors and windows, along with a 3D model.

The values the app provides are approximations obtained from sensors and may contain errors. Their accuracy depends on lighting, surface materials, objects in the room and how the scan is performed. They must not be used as the only source for construction, manufacturing, contracting, structural calculations, installations or any purpose that requires certified accuracy.
